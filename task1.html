About Us

The Riverside Community Club is a volunteer-run gathering space for people of all ages. We host classes, clubs, and events throughout the year.

"This club has been the heart of our street for two decades."

— Local resident

Our Programs

We currently offer the following programs:

  • Beginner pottery
  • Youth chess club
  • Community garden
  • Senior book circle

How to Join

  1. Fill out the contact form below
  2. Attend a free trial session
  3. Pay the small annual membership fee
  4. Show up and enjoy!
